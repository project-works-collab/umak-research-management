# UMak-RMLIS Terms and Conditions

**Final academic submission — proposed policy pending institutional adoption.**

Prepared: 21 November 2026. Effective date: **[pending institutional approval]**.

These proposed terms explain the access, use, and administration of the **University of Makati Research Management and Lifecycle Information System (UMak-RMLIS)**, also referred to as the CRM application. It covers student researchers, faculty researchers, review committee members, and CRM administrative staff. The proposed operator is the **University of Makati (UMak)** through its Center for Research Management (CRM); **[the authorized legal entity, responsible office, and approval authority must be confirmed]**. The current repository is a synthetic-data teaching implementation, not an operational university service.

---

## 1. Eligibility, Account Provisioning, and Access Rules

1. **Authorized User Roles:** Access to authenticated services in UMak-RMLIS is intended for:
   - Enrolled undergraduate and graduate student researchers;
   - Faculty researchers, thesis and dissertation advisers;
   - Appointed peer reviewers and evaluation committee members;
   - Authorized administrative and technical personnel of the CRM.
2. **Account Provisioning:**
   - **Researcher Self-Registration:** Student and faculty researchers self-register using their institutional email address (`@umak.edu.ph`). Identity ownership is verified through an email verification code prior to account activation.
   - **Staff and Administrator Accounts:** Staff and reviewer accounts cannot be self-registered. They are provisioned and assigned granular permissions (`content_management`, `request_processing`, `registry_management`, `progress_updates`, `reporting`, `account_administration`) exclusively by authorized CRM account administrators.
3. **Public Catalogue Access:** Public users may browse and search approved catalogue information (research titles, abstracts, keywords, academic year, department, authors) without an account or login.
4. **Minors and Advising:** Student researchers under the age of majority participate under the academic guidance of their designated faculty advisers or department chairs, pursuant to the Education Act of 1982 (B.P. Blg. 232).
5. **Access Boundaries:** Users must access only records and functions authorized for their role. Impersonating another person, using another's credentials, or attempting unauthorized privilege escalation is strictly prohibited.

---

## 2. Account Responsibilities and User Obligations

1. **Accuracy of Information:** In alignment with UMak institutional policy (UMak Privacy Notice, Client Obligations), users are obligated to provide complete, accurate, and truthful academic information. Submitting false, fraudulent, or fabricated information invalidates the processing of the transaction and may result in administrative or disciplinary proceedings under approved university rules.
2. **Credential Confidentiality:** Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and active session tokens. Do not share account passwords or verification codes with others.
3. **Device and Session Security:** Users must take reasonable precautions to secure devices used to access the service or local offline caches (such as device lock screens and logging out after sessions on shared computers).
4. **Reporting Compromise:** Suspected unauthorized access or account compromise should be reported promptly to **[the confirmed institutional support channel]** (coursework contact: `research@umak.edu.ph`).

---

## 3. Data Privacy and Confidentiality Covenants

Pursuant to Section 7 of the UMak Data Privacy Policy Statement (*Compliance with Data Privacy Clauses on Contracts*), compliance with privacy rules is a condition of system access:

1. **Protection of Personal Data:** Users must respect the privacy rights of data subjects. Unlawful scraping, harvesting, or unauthorized disclosure of personal data of researchers, faculty, advisers, or reviewers is strictly prohibited.
2. **Human Subject Research Compliance:** When submitting manuscripts or datasets, researchers must ensure that personal data of human research participants has been collected with verified informed consent and processed under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Appropriate ethics clearance must be obtained from the **UMak Research Ethics Committee (UMak-REC)** prior to submission, and participant data must be anonymized or redacted where appropriate.
3. **Confidentiality of Reviews and School Records:**
   - In compliance with B.P. Blg. 232 (Section 9, Item 4), student academic records and evaluative submissions are confidential.
   - Reviewers and advisers must maintain strict confidentiality regarding draft manuscripts and evaluation deliberations assigned to them.
   - Temporary manuscript access links are for authorized evaluators only and must not be shared or redistributed.

---

## 4. Research Submissions, Intellectual Property, and Licences

1. **Authorship and Ownership:** In accordance with the **Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 8293)** and the approved University of Makati Intellectual Property Policy, authors retain moral rights and copyright in their original research proposals, manuscripts, theses, and dissertations. Submission to UMak-RMLIS does not transfer copyright ownership to the operator.
2. **Limited Institutional Processing Permission:** Authors grant the University of Makati a non-exclusive, royalty-free institutional permission strictly necessary to:
   - Store, replicate, format, and back up submissions within secure university infrastructure;
   - Transmit manuscripts to assigned faculty advisers, panel members, and peer reviewers for official academic evaluation;
   - Subject submissions to automated format and similarity checks;
   - Archive approved manuscripts in the institutional repository pursuant to the **National Archives of the Philippines Act of 2007 (RA 9470)**.
3. **Public Catalogue Publication:** Upon formal academic approval, clearance, and CRM recommendation, catalogue metadata (title, abstract, keywords, authors, academic year, and department) is published in the public registry. Full manuscripts remain restricted unless separate written authorization for open-access dissemination is executed.
4. **Third-Party Rights and Originality:** Authors warrant that their submissions are original, do not infringe third-party rights, and comply with institutional academic integrity standards.

---

## 5. Acceptable Use and Cybercrime Prevention (RA 10175)

Users agree to use the service exclusively for authorized academic, research, and administrative purposes. Pursuant to Section 4 of the **Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10175)**, the following acts are strictly prohibited:

1. **Illegal Access:** Accessing or attempting to access any server, database, or system endpoint without authorization or beyond assigned role privileges.
2. **Illegal Interception:** Intercepting non-public transmissions of data, manuscripts, session tokens, or system communications.
3. **Data Interference:** Unauthorized altering, damaging, deleting, corrupting, suppressing, or deteriorating of research records, evaluation feedback, audit logs, or system files.
4. **System Interference:** Hindering or disrupting platform operation, overloading infrastructure, or deploying denial-of-service attacks.
5. **Malicious Code:** Introducing viruses, Trojan horses, worms, ransomware, or disguised executables.
6. **Cyber-Falsification:** Forging administrative approvals, signatures, or evaluation outcomes.
7. **Security Probing:** Performing penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, or automated probing against UMak-RMLIS without prior, formal written authorization from the CRM and IT administration.

## 6. Document Upload Limits, Formats, and Consultation Queues

1. **File Formats and Upload Limits:**
   - **Service Request Attachments (Intake Forms):** Permitted in authentic PDF or DOCX format, up to **10 MB per file, maximum five (5) files per request**, as established in Requirements Engineering.
   - **Manuscript Document Repository Uploads:** Permitted in authentic PDF or DOCX format, up to **25,000,000 bytes (~25 MB) per document file**, as enforced by backend document storage validation.
   - **Harmonization Notice:** **[Institutional confirmation is required to harmonize and establish unified storage limits across all intake channels.]**
2. **Automated Inspection:** Uploaded files undergo format verification, MIME-type checks, and security scanning. Password-protected archives, macros, and invalid structures are rejected.
3. **Consultation Requests and Administrative Service Delivery:** Consultation requests for research advising, statistical consultation, or manuscript formatting are scheduled and queued in accordance with the **Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018 (Republic Act No. 11032)**. Priority indicators and requested deadlines assist CRM staff in organizing workflows but do not guarantee instant completion without requisite academic deliberation.
4. **Separation of Administrative Notes:** Internal staff notes and reviewer deliberations are restricted administrative records and are not part of researcher-facing feedback views.

---

## 7. Automated Decision-Support Tools and Algorithmic Limitations

1. **Similarity and Keyword Matching:** The platform provides automated title and keyword similarity checking. Users acknowledge that algorithmic similarity scores:
   - Are advisory decision-support indicators designed to assist human review;
   - Do not constitute conclusive proof of plagiarism, duplication, or academic misconduct;
   - Do not guarantee novelty or originality;
   - Are subject to final qualitative evaluation by faculty advisers, review panels, and the CRM.
2. **Workflow Readiness Estimates:** Automated duration estimations and workflow milestone calculations are mathematical approximations based on typical prerequisites and capacity models. They do not constitute a guarantee of academic completion dates or replace official graduation clearances.

---

## 8. Privacy, Records Retention, and Account Deletion

1. **Privacy Notice:** The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) details data categories, lawful bases, optional choices, and data subject rights under RA 10173.
2. **Retention Schedules:** **[Retention periods and disposal rules for accounts, evaluation records, and manuscripts remain subject to National Archives of the Philippines (RA 9470) guidelines and institutional approval.]**
3. **Reviewed Deletion Scope:** Account deletion requests undergo staff review to protect academic integrity holds and shared-study coauthor rights. Approved live deletion removes database records, but does not promise instantaneous purging of offline disaster-recovery backups or older object versions. Restored backups must be reconciled against deletion audit records before service resumes.

---

## 9. Platform Availability and Mobile Offline Cache

1. **System Uptime:** Services may be interrupted by maintenance, emergency security updates, or connectivity issues. No uptime or delivery warranty is established by these academic terms. **[Outage-support channels and alternative submission procedures must be confirmed.]**
2. **Mobile Library and Offline Caching:** The UMak-RMLIS mobile application provides local encrypted storage for active sessions and cached public catalogue data to support offline viewing. Offline data is read-only and may become desynchronized; users must establish online connectivity to perform authoritative submissions, check formal evaluation status, or synchronize account records.

---

## 10. Violations, Disciplinary Processes, and Applicable Rules

1. **Compliance with University Codes:** Suspected violations of these terms, academic dishonesty, unauthorized disclosures, or cyber offenses will be addressed through approved institutional procedures:
   - For students: The UMak Student Handbook and academic integrity policies;
   - For faculty and employees: The UMak Faculty Code, Civil Service Commission rules, and Republic Act No. 6713 (Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees);
   - Statutory compliance: Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012).
2. **Proportionate Administrative Actions:** The university may apply proportionate administrative measures, such as temporary credential suspension or invalidation of fraudulent requests, subject to established procedural due process.
3. **No Invented Sanctions:** These terms do not create new criminal penalties, private tribunals, or unapproved disciplinary sanctions. Applicable university codes and Philippine statutory laws govern all enforcement actions.
