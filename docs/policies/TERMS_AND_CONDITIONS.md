# UMak-RMLIS Terms and Conditions

**Final academic submission — proposed policy pending institutional adoption.**

Prepared: 10 November 2026. Effective date: **[pending institutional approval]**.

These proposed terms explain use of the University of Makati Research Management and Library Information System (UMak-RMLIS), also referred to as the CRM application, by student researchers, faculty researchers and CRM staff. The proposed operator is the University of Makati through its Center for Research Management; **[the authorized legal entity, responsible office and approval authority must be confirmed]**. The current repository is a synthetic-data teaching implementation, not an operational university service.

## 1. Eligibility and authorized access

Use an account issued or approved for your role and assigned duties. **[Eligibility, account provisioning and any procedure for minors require institutional approval.]** These terms do not establish a universal minimum age of 16.

Researchers may access their own profiles and studies in which they have authorized membership. Assigned evaluation staff may review the private studies assigned to them. Do not use another person's account, impersonate another role or attempt to access records outside your authorization. Public access is limited to approved catalogue information and published service material.

## 2. Account responsibilities

Keep credentials and session information private, use a secure device and sign out when finished. Report suspected account compromise through **[the confirmed support or incident channel]**. Keep submitted account and research information accurate, and request correction where needed. Sharing a temporary manuscript link does not grant another person permission to use it.

## 3. Research submissions and intellectual property

Submit only material that you are authorized to provide. Respect coauthor rights, participant privacy, confidentiality obligations, research-ethics approvals and any applicable third-party licence. Submission does not transfer ownership of research or manuscripts to the operator.

The proposed permission is limited to storing, protecting, backing up and making necessary technical copies of submissions, and making them available to authorized members and assigned reviewers for the requested research service. It does not permit unrelated commercialization, broad derivative works or unrestricted public redistribution. **[Institutional IP rules and the authority to grant these processing permissions must be confirmed.]**

Public catalogue approval covers the approved title, abstract, year and department. It must follow an authorized publication process; it does not publish the manuscript. Separate permission is required for any wider release or reuse. Review feedback and internal staff notes have different access restrictions.

Software and branding rights remain subject to applicable ownership and licensing arrangements. These terms do not override an existing repository licence or establish ownership that has not been confirmed by the institution.

## 4. Acceptable use

- Use the service for authorized research and administration.
- Do not attempt unauthorized access, privilege escalation, interference with records or disruption of service.
- Do not upload malware, disguised executables, unlawfully obtained data or material that infringes another person's rights.
- Do not disclose private manuscripts, internal notes, credentials or personal information without authority.
- Do not submit deliberately false information, impersonate others or use the service for harassment, phishing or spam.
- Perform security testing only with explicit authorization and an agreed scope.

These rules do not create new criminal penalties or university disciplinary sanctions. Applicable law and approved institutional procedures govern those matters.

## 5. Files, feedback and consultation requests

Where upload services are enabled, submissions must be valid PDF or DOCX files of no more than **25,000,000 bytes per file**. File inspection, malware scanning and rate or capacity limits may reject or delay a submission. An unsuccessful upload is not evidence of receipt. A stored file or successful request is not academic approval.

Check the recorded outcome and keep an appropriate copy of your work. Contact support to resolve submission errors or disputed feedback. Internal staff notes are restricted administrative records and are not part of the researcher-facing feedback response.

Consultation requests record the service requested and scheduling information. Deadline-based ordering helps staff organize work; it does not guarantee an appointment, response time or completion before a deadline. **[The institution must approve scheduling, escalation and alternative service procedures.]**

## 6. Automated results and their limits

Title and keyword similarity results are suggestions for human review. A high score does not prove plagiarism, duplication or misconduct, and a low score does not prove originality. Current deployed-service availability and each algorithm's actual integration must be checked separately from notebook or benchmark results.

Workflow-readiness and duration calculations depend on supplied prerequisites, completion states, evidence gates and duration estimates. Dependency-based estimates assume unlimited parallel capacity; they do not guarantee completion dates or account for every staffing or external delay. Receipt of evidence does not automatically mean that staff have verified it. Authorized people remain responsible for academic and administrative decisions.

## 7. Privacy and deletion

The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) describes data categories, purposes, access, optional preferences, rights and reviewed deletion. Optional consent choices are separate from these terms. A deletion request requires review and does not guarantee immediate removal from backups, shared records or older object versions.

## 8. Availability and mobile access

Some functions require connectivity and configured backend services. Maintenance, security restrictions, unavailable dependencies or network problems may interrupt access. No uptime or delivery guarantee is established by these academic terms. **[The outage-support channel and alternative submission route must be confirmed.]**

The supplied mobile library supports cached public material and protected session storage; it is not a complete released application. Offline material can be outdated and does not confirm successful submission. Follow institutional instructions for approved client versions when a client is released.

## 9. Restrictions, complaints and review

The proposed institutional process is to assess reported misuse, use proportionate protective measures, explain restrictions where appropriate and provide a way to request review. Urgent containment may be necessary to protect people or records. **[Authorized decision-makers, notice, review, appeal and restoration procedures must be approved.]** These clauses do not claim an implemented moderation, account-suspension or in-app appeals workflow.

Proposed research-office contact supplied for this coursework: **research@umak.edu.ph**. **[Confirm its support role and insert the authorized escalation channel before adoption.]** Do not send sensitive attachments or credentials to an unconfirmed channel.

## 10. Adoption, changes and applicable rules

These terms are not yet effective and do not assert that using or downloading the prototype creates institutional acceptance. **[The institution must approve an acknowledgment process, version and effective date.]** No dedicated terms-acceptance workflow is established by publishing this file.

Philippine law and applicable approved university policies inform institutional review. These academic terms do not create charges, ownership transfers, liability waivers, indemnities or a foreign legal representative. Any material changes must be communicated through an approved process before taking effect, with renewed agreement where required.
