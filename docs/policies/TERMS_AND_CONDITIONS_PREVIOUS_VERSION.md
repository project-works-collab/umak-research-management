# UMak-RMLIS Terms and Conditions

**University of Makati — Center for Research Management**  
**Research Management and Library Information System (UMak-RMLIS / CRM Application)**

Prepared: 21 November 2026. Effective date: **[pending institutional approval]**.

These Terms and Conditions govern the access, use, and administration of the **University of Makati Research Management and Library Information System (UMak-RMLIS)**, also known as the CRM application. The platform is operated by the **University of Makati (UMak)**, an institution of higher learning established and funded by the City Government of Makati operating under Makati City Ordinance 2020-059 (Revised UMak Charter), through its **Center for Research Management (CRM)** in coordination with the **Information Technology Center (ITC)** and the **Center for Data Protection and Records Management Services (CDPRMS)**.

By accessing, registering with, or utilizing the UMak-RMLIS platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these Terms and Conditions, the [UMak-RMLIS Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md), the UMak Data Privacy Policy Statement, the UMak Student Handbook, the UMak Faculty Code, and all applicable Philippine statutory laws and university regulations.

---

## 1. Eligibility, Account Provisioning, and Authorized Roles

1. **Eligible Users:** Access to UMak-RMLIS is strictly restricted to authorized members of the University of Makati academic community, specifically:
   - Currently enrolled undergraduate and graduate student researchers;
   - Active faculty researchers, thesis and dissertation advisers;
   - Appointed peer reviewers and evaluation panel members;
   - Authorized administrative and technical personnel of the CRM and ITC.
2. **Account Provisioning:** Accounts are provisioned and authenticated through official institutional email credentials (`@umak.edu.ph`). Users must access only the modules and roles corresponding to their verified academic appointment and assigned duties.
3. **Minors and Student Protections:** In accordance with the Education Act of 1982 (B.P. Blg. 232) and applicable laws, student researchers under the age of majority shall access the system under the supervision and guidance of their designated academic advisers or department chairs.
4. **Access Restrictions:** Impersonation of another researcher, reviewer, or administrator, as well as unauthorized attempts to escalate privileges, access private studies, or bypass role-based access controls (RBAC), is strictly prohibited and constitutes a major administrative violation.

---

## 2. User Obligations and Account Responsibilities

1. **Accuracy of Information:** In alignment with UMak institutional policy (UMak Privacy Notice, Section on Client Obligations), users are legally and institutionally obligated to provide complete, true, accurate, and current information. Any willful submission of false or misleading data, forged credentials, fabricated research metadata, or unauthorized proxy submissions will invalidate the transaction and subject the user to academic, administrative, and legal sanctions.
2. **Credential Confidentiality:** Users are solely responsible for safeguarding their login credentials, passwords, two-factor authentication methods, and active session tokens. Sharing account credentials with other students, faculty, or external parties is strictly prohibited.
3. **Device Security:** Users must ensure that devices used to access the web portal or mobile application (including offline cache copies) are secured with passcodes, biometrics, or encryption. Users must log out promptly upon concluding a session on shared or public workstations.
4. **Reporting Security Concerns:** Users must immediately notify the CRM (`research@umak.edu.ph`) and the DPO (`dprms@umak.edu.ph`) of any suspected account compromise, unauthorized access, or loss of an authenticated device.

---

## 3. Data Privacy and Confidentiality Covenants

Pursuant to Section 7 of the UMak Data Privacy Policy Statement (*Compliance with Data Privacy Clauses on Contracts*), compliance with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)** is a binding contractual condition of platform use:

1. **Protection of Personal Data:** Users shall respect the privacy rights of all data subjects whose information is processed on the platform. Users shall not harvest, harvest-scrape, extract, distribute, or unlawfully disclose the names, email addresses, contact details, or personal data of student researchers, faculty, advisers, or reviewers.
2. **Human Subject Research Compliance:** When submitting manuscripts, datasets, or abstracts, researchers must ensure that all personal information or sensitive personal information concerning human participants has been collected with verified informed consent and processed in accordance with RA 10173, university research ethics standards, and approvals issued by the **UMak Research Ethics Committee (UMak-REC)**. Unnecessary or identifying personal data of research subjects must be anonymized or redacted prior to upload.
3. **Confidentiality of Reviews and School Records:** In compliance with B.P. Blg. 232 (Section 9, Item 4) and university academic policy:
   - Unpublished manuscripts, research proposals, reviewer deliberations, and internal CRM administrative notes are confidential academic records;
   - Reviewers and advisers must preserve strict confidentiality regarding manuscripts assigned to them and must not disclose, disseminate, or appropriate ideas contained therein;
   - Temporary manuscript access links are for the sole use of authorized evaluators and must not be forwarded or published.

## 4. Research Submissions, Intellectual Property, and Licences

1. **Authorship and Ownership:** Pursuant to the **Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 8293)** and the UMak Intellectual Property Policy, student and faculty researchers retain moral rights and copyright ownership over their original research proposals, manuscripts, theses, and dissertations.
2. **Limited Institutional Licence:** By submitting research materials to UMak-RMLIS, authors grant the University of Makati a non-exclusive, royalty-free, institutional licence to:
   - Store, replicate, index, format, and host the submissions within secure university systems and backup repositories;
   - Transmit manuscripts to assigned faculty advisers, panel members, and peer reviewers for official academic evaluation;
   - Process manuscripts through automated similarity assessment and structural verification tools;
   - Archive approved manuscripts in the university's permanent repository in compliance with archival obligations under the **National Archives of the Philippines Act of 2007 (RA 9470)**.
3. **Public Catalogue Release:** Authors understand that upon formal institutional clearance, thesis defense approval, and recommendation by the CRM, catalogue metadata (research title, abstract, keywords, authors, academic year, and college) will be published in the university's public research registry. Full manuscripts remain protected and restricted unless separate written authorization for open-access release is executed.
4. **Third-Party Rights and Plagiarism:** Authors warrant that their submissions are original, do not infringe upon any third-party copyright, patent, trademark, or trade secret, and comply fully with academic integrity standards. Plagiarism, data fabrication, or falsification will trigger disciplinary proceedings under university codes.

## 5. Acceptable Use and Cybercrime Prevention (RA 10175)

Users agree to use UMak-RMLIS solely for authorized academic, research, and administrative purposes. Pursuant to Section 4 of the **Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10175)**, the following actions are strictly prohibited:

1. **Illegal Access:** Accessing or attempting to access any portion of the system, server, database, or network without authorization or beyond assigned role privileges.
2. **Illegal Interception:** Intercepting non-public transmissions of personal data, manuscripts, session tokens, or system communications without authorization.
3. **Data Interference:** Unauthorized altering, damaging, deleting, corrupting, suppressing, or deteriorating of research records, manuscripts, evaluation scores, audit logs, or system files.
4. **System Interference:** Hindering or disrupting the operation of UMak-RMLIS, overloading infrastructure, deploying denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, or generating automated request storms.
5. **Misuse of Devices and Malicious Code:** Uploading or introducing software viruses, worms, Trojan horses, ransomware, web shells, or disguised executables.
6. **Cyber-Falsification:** Submitting fraudulent documents, forging digital approval signatures, or misrepresenting official evaluation results.
7. **Unauthorized Security Testing:** Conducting penetration testing, automated vulnerability scanning, or security probing against UMak-RMLIS without prior, formal written authorization from the CRM and the Information Technology Center (ITC).

## 6. Document Upload Limits, Formats, and Consultation Services

1. **Permitted File Formats and Size:** Where document upload services are activated, files must be authentic, uncorrupted documents in **PDF (.pdf)** or **Word (.docx)** format. The maximum permissible file size is strictly **25,000,000 bytes (25 MB)** per submission.
2. **Automated Inspection:** Uploaded files are subject to automated verification, MIME-type validation, and antivirus scanning. The university reserves the right to reject or quarantine files that fail inspection or exceed capacity thresholds.
3. **Consultation Requests and Administrative Service Delivery:** Consultation requests for research advising, statistical consultation, or manuscript formatting are scheduled and queued in accordance with the **Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018 (Republic Act No. 11032)**. Priority indicators and requested deadlines assist CRM staff in organizing workflows but do not guarantee instant completion without requisite academic deliberation.
4. **Administrative Records Separation:** Internal administrative notes, reviewer coordination records, and staff queue logs are privileged institutional records and are excluded from researcher-facing feedback views.

---

## 7. Automated Decision-Support Tools and Algorithmic Limitations

1. **Similarity and Keyword Matching:** The platform provides automated title and keyword similarity checking. Users acknowledge that algorithmic similarity scores:
   - Are advisory decision-support indicators designed to assist human review;
   - Do not constitute conclusive proof of plagiarism, duplication, or academic misconduct;
   - Do not guarantee novelty or originality;
   - Are subject to final qualitative evaluation by faculty advisers, review panels, and the CRM.
2. **Workflow Readiness Estimates:** Automated duration estimations and workflow milestone calculations are mathematical approximations based on typical prerequisites and capacity models. They do not constitute a guarantee of academic completion dates or replace official graduation clearances.

---

## 8. Records Retention, Archival Preservation, and Account Deletion

1. **Archival Preservation (RA 9470):** Approved research titles, graduation clearances, and academic registry entries form part of the official public record of the University of Makati and are preserved pursuant to the **National Archives of the Philippines Act of 2007 (RA 9470)** and university archival schedules.
2. **Account Deletion Review:** Registered users may apply for account deletion pursuant to their rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. All deletion requests undergo formal administrative review by the CRM and CDPRMS. Deletion will not be executed if:
   - There are pending academic disciplinary, integrity, or financial hold proceedings;
   - The user is a co-author on multi-party research that requires record retention for remaining co-authors;
   - Retention is mandated by RA 9470 or other legal and regulatory obligations.
3. **Disaster Recovery and Archival Backups:** Following approved live account deletion, historical backup snapshots are recycled according to the IT disaster recovery schedule. Re-application of deletion records ensures restored data maintains deleted account status.

## 9. Platform Availability and Mobile Offline Cache

1. **System Uptime:** The university strives to maintain platform reliability through the Information Technology Center (ITC) enterprise infrastructure (including Firewalls, DRaaS, and redundant data centers). However, services may be temporarily suspended for scheduled maintenance, emergency security patches, or telecommunications outages. UMak provides no warranty of uninterrupted availability.
2. **Mobile Library and Offline Caching:** The UMak-RMLIS mobile application provides local encrypted storage for active sessions and cached public catalogue data to support offline viewing. Offline data is read-only and may become desynchronized; users must establish online connectivity to perform authoritative submissions, check formal evaluation status, or synchronize account records.

## 10. Violations, Disciplinary Sanctions, and Due Process

1. **Enforcement and Penalties:** Any violation of these Terms and Conditions, unauthorized disclosure of confidential data, cybercrime offenses, or academic dishonesty will be investigated and penalized in accordance with:
   - The UMak Student Handbook (for student researchers);
   - The UMak Faculty Code and Civil Service Commission Rules (for faculty and staff);
   - Republic Act No. 6713 (Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees);
   - Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012).
2. **Disciplinary Measures:** Measures may include temporary account suspension, invalidation of research submissions, referral to the Student Disciplinary Tribunal or Human Resource Management Office (HRMO), withholding of academic clearance, expulsion, employment termination, and civil or criminal prosecution.
3. **Due Process:** In all investigations and administrative proceedings, the university adheres to procedural due process, ensuring affected individuals are notified in writing and afforded reasonable opportunity to present their side and submit evidence.
